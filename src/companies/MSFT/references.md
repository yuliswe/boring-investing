# MSFT — Data sources

## Primary filings

- [Microsoft Investor Relations](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor) —
  annual reports, 10-K, 10-Q, earnings press releases.
- [SEC EDGAR — MSFT filings](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000789019&type=10-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=10) —
  10-K annual reports filed with the SEC.

## Segment and financial data

- [Microsoft FY24 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/789019/000078901924000017/msft-20240630.htm) —
  source for FY24 revenue, operating income, and segment breakdown
  (Productivity & Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, More Personal Computing).
- [Microsoft FY23 Annual Report](https://www.microsoft.com/investor/reports/ar23/) —
  source for FY23 figures and year-over-year comparisons.

## Market data

- [Yahoo Finance — MSFT](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/MSFT/) —
  market capitalisation snapshot.

## Notes

- All revenue and operating-income figures in `data/financials.json` and
  `data/segments.json` are in billions of USD and rounded to the nearest billion.
- Figures are fiscal-year values (Microsoft's fiscal year ends 30 June).
