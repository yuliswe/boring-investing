# TOI — Data sources

## Revenue

Annual revenue figures (FY20–FY25) in billions of euros are taken from the
annual press releases published on
[GlobeNewsWire](https://www.globenewswire.com/) and cross-checked against
[Stock Analysis](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/tsxv/TOI/revenue/). Values
match the consolidated revenue reported in Topicus's annual shareholder
reports filed on SEDAR+.

Revenue breakdown by type (licence, professional services, hardware & other,
maintenance & recurring) is from the consolidated income statements in the
annual press releases.

## Derived series

P/FCF = year-end market cap ÷ FCFA2S. Market cap = share price (CAD) ×
shares outstanding, converted to EUR using approximate year-end CAD/EUR
exchange rates. FCFA2S per share = FCFA2S ÷ diluted shares outstanding
(~129.8M throughout). FCF margin = FCFA2S ÷ revenue.

## Notes

- All dollar figures in `data/financials.ts` are in billions of EUR, rounded
  to three decimals.
- Figures are calendar-year values (Topicus's fiscal year ends 31 December).
- Topicus reports under IFRS. Amortization of acquired intangible assets is a
  material non-cash charge that depresses reported net income; the P/FCF ratio
  (using FCFA2S) better reflects the company's cash economics.
- The FY21 net loss of €2.2 billion was caused by a non-cash redeemable
  preferred securities charge at IPO and does not reflect operating
  performance.
- FY25 net income of €70 million was depressed by a €222 million equity method
  revaluation charge related to the Asseco Poland investment.
