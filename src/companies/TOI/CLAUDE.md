# TOI — Topicus.com Inc.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Business model.** Topicus.com is a majority-owned subsidiary of
  Constellation Software Inc. (CSU), operating the same serial-acquisition
  playbook focused on European vertical market software businesses. Revenue
  growth is primarily acquisition-driven, with organic growth typically in the
  low-to-mid single digits.

- **Relationship with CSU.** Topicus was formed in February 2021 when CSU
  completed the IPO of Topicus.com Inc. on the TSX Venture Exchange. CSU
  retains majority economic interest through subordinate voting shares and
  continues to consolidate Topicus in its financial statements. The 2021
  redeemable preferred securities charge of €2.3 billion was a non-cash item
  related to the conversion of these instruments at IPO.

- **Fiscal year.** Topicus uses a calendar fiscal year ending 31 December, so
  "FY25" covers January through December 2025. Financial data starts from FY20,
  the last full year before the IPO, because earlier periods reflect a different
  corporate structure.

- **Currency.** Financial statements are reported in euros. The company is
  headquartered in Deventer, the Netherlands, and listed on the TSX Venture
  Exchange in Canadian dollars.

- **IFRS reporting and intangible amortization.** Topicus reports under IFRS,
  which requires amortization of acquired intangible assets. This large
  non-cash charge significantly depresses reported net income relative to cash
  earnings. P/FCF (using FCFA2S) is a more representative valuation metric
  than P/E. The company's preferred profitability measure is "free cash flow
  available to shareholders" (FCFA2S).

- **FCFA2S.** Free cash flow available to shareholders is the company's
  preferred metric, which adjusts operating cash flow for capital expenditures,
  lease payments, and the portion attributable to non-controlling interests.
  The values in the data files use FCFA2S as reported in the annual press
  releases.

- **Expense reporting.** Topicus reports expenses by nature under IFRS (staff,
  hardware, third-party, etc.), not by function (COGS, SG&A, R&D). R&D
  spending is not separately disclosed. The company does not report segment-
  level profitability by operating group.

- **Asseco Poland investment (2025).** In 2025, Topicus acquired a significant
  stake in Asseco Poland for approximately €385 million. The equity method
  revaluation charge of €222 million and related fair value adjustments
  depressed FY25 net income to €70 million, despite strong underlying
  operating performance and FCFA2S growth of 23%.
