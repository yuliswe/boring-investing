# HD — The Home Depot, Inc.

Analytical method: [retail](../../methods/retail.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Fiscal year.** Home Depot uses a 52/53-week fiscal year ending on the
  Sunday nearest 31 January, and names each year by the calendar year in
  which it starts, so "FY25" is the year ended 1 February 2026. FY18 and FY24
  had 53 weeks. The extra week added about $1.7B and $2.5B of net sales, and
  comparable sales exclude it.

- **Acquisitions change the revenue base.** HD Supply (December 2020, $8.7B),
  SRS (June 2024, $18.0B) and GMS (September 2025, $5.1B) are inside net
  sales, margins and the cash flow statement from their closing dates.
  Comparable sales, transactions, average ticket, online share and sales per
  square foot exclude them. SRS and GMS make up the "Other" segment and the
  Other (SRS) product line. Mingledorff's (May 2026, about $1.1B) closed after
  the last fiscal year shown.

- **Metric substitutions.** The retail method's "revenue share of the growth
  market" is replaced by customer transactions and average ticket, because
  Home Depot's store base is mature and its growth comes from acquisitions
  rather than an expansion region. "Revenue per average store" is replaced by
  reported sales per retail square foot, because consolidated revenue
  includes SRS and GMS, which have no stores. Home Depot stopped reporting
  sales per square foot in the FY25 10-K, so that series ends at FY24.

- **Net debt and small equity.** Decades of buybacks left stockholders'
  equity small and negative in FY18, FY19 and FY21, so ROE is meaningless and
  the page uses lease-adjusted ROIC. Net cash is negative in every year.
  Buybacks stopped after the SRS deal, and the FY25 10-K says they will not
  resume in FY26.

- **Product-line regroupings.** Departments moved between Building
  Materials, Décor and Hardlines in FY19, Q1 FY24 and Q1 FY25. The Revenue
  Streams chart uses the latest restated figure for each year, which leaves
  FY17–FY21 on older groupings.

- **Forward estimates.** FY26E comes from management guidance, issued
  24 February 2026 and reaffirmed on 19 May and 18 August 2026. Net sales of
  $170.45B are FY25 sales grown by the 3.5% midpoint of 2.5–4.5% growth.
  Comparable sales growth (1.0%), gross margin (33.1%), operating margin
  (12.5%, giving $21.31B of operating income), new stores (15) and capital
  expenditures (2.5% of sales, $4.26B) are also guidance midpoints. Diluted
  EPS of $14.51 is the midpoint of flat to 4% growth from $14.23. Net earnings
  of $14.39B are derived as (operating income − $2.3B guided net interest) ×
  (1 − 24.3% guided tax rate). Home Depot gives no cash flow guidance, so
  FY26E free cash flow is the stockanalysis.com consensus of $16.97B, which
  is divided by FY25 diluted shares (995M) for the per-share figure. The
  forward P/E and P/FCF recalculate when the user adjusts the price.
