# NFLX — Data Sources

## SEC filings

- **FY2024 10-K** (filed 31 Jan 2025): revenue, operating income, expense
  breakdown, balance sheet, and cash-flow data for FY22–FY24. Regional
  revenue disclosure for FY22–FY24.
- **FY2021 10-K**: income statement and balance sheet for FY21.
- **FY2020 10-K**: income statement, balance sheet, and regional revenue for
  FY18–FY20.
- **FY2017 10-K**: income statement and balance sheet for FY15–FY17.
- **FY2025 10-K** (filed 23 Jan 2026): revenue and operating income for FY25.

## Earnings letters

- Quarterly earnings letters (Q4 2017 through Q4 2024) for global paid
  membership counts at period end.

## Market data

- Year-end closing prices sourced from Yahoo Finance, adjusted for the
  10-for-1 stock split on 14 November 2025.
- P/E, P/FCF, and PEG computed from year-end closing prices and trailing
  annual EPS / FCF per share / EPS growth.

## Guidance

- FY26E revenue of $50.7–51.7 B (midpoint $51.2 B) and operating margin
  ~31.5% from the Q4 2025 earnings letter.
- FY26E operating income of $16.13 B derived from the 31.5% margin guidance
  on $51.2 B midpoint revenue.
- FY26E regional split is estimated proportionally from FY24–FY25 growth
  trends.

## Consensus estimates

- FY26E consensus from stockanalysis.com (46 analysts, retrieved 22 Sep 2026):
  revenue $51.22 B, EPS $3.59, net income $15.27 B, free cash flow $12.87 B.
- FY26E free cash flow per share ($3.02) derived from consensus FCF of
  $12.87 B divided by estimated diluted shares of ~4.25 B (implied by
  consensus net income ÷ consensus EPS).
- FY26E P/E, P/FCF, and PEG computed from the 21 Sep 2026 closing price of
  $73.36.
- Balance-sheet-dependent metrics (ROE, ROIC, D/E, SGR) are left null for
  FY26E because Netflix's large share repurchase program ($25 B authorized
  in Apr 2026) makes year-end equity projections unreliable.

## Notes

- D&A figures represent depreciation of property and equipment only; content
  amortisation is included in cost of revenue.
- Stock-based compensation sourced from the cash-flow statement.
- Free cash flow = operating cash flow − capital expenditures, sourced from
  annual cash-flow statements.
- Blended ARM = total annual revenue ÷ average paid memberships ÷ 12.
