# SHOP — Shopify Inc.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Segments.** Shopify reports a single operating segment. The Revenue
  Streams section splits revenue into subscription solutions and merchant
  solutions, as the income statement does. No FY26E split is shown because
  Shopify guides one quarter at a time and analysts publish only total
  revenue.

- **Fiscal year.** Shopify's fiscal year is the calendar year, so "FY25"
  covers January through December 2025.

- **Filing forms.** Shopify is a Canadian company that filed annual reports
  on Form 40-F through FY23 and on Form 10-K from FY24. Both use US GAAP and
  US dollars. Quarterly reports were 6-Ks until it began filing 10-Qs.

- **Stock split.** A ten-for-one split took effect in June 2022. FY17 to FY19
  EPS and share counts are the as-filed figures divided by ten; FY20 diluted
  EPS ($2.59 as filed) is likewise divided by ten. Yahoo Finance year-end
  prices are already split-adjusted.

- **Equity investment swings.** GAAP net income includes mark-to-market gains
  and losses on stakes in Affirm, Global-E, Klaviyo and private companies,
  and Shopify's share of Flexport's results. These dominate FY21 (+$2.86B),
  FY22 (−$2.87B), FY23 (+$1.36B) and FY24 (+$0.85B), so P/E, ROE and net
  margin are noisy. Free cash flow is the steadier measure.

- **Logistics sale.** In the second quarter of 2023 Shopify sold Shopify
  Logistics and Deliverr to Flexport for mostly non-cash consideration,
  recording a $1.34B impairment in operating expenses. FY23 operating income
  and ROIC include it.

- **Presentation changes.** Transaction and loan losses were folded into
  general and administrative expenses until FY18 and are shown separately
  from FY19 (the FY20 40-F restates FY19). Merchant loans moved to investing
  cash flows from FY21, and merchant cash advances followed in April 2026.

- **FY26E.** Revenue, net income and free cash flow are S&P Global
  consensus. GAAP operating income is built from nine-month actuals plus
  Q4 guidance, because the consensus operating income is non-GAAP.

- **Default price.** The default price is an intraday quote from
  3 November 2026, the day Q3 results were released, when the stock was down
  about 7%. Update it to a close when the page is next refreshed.
