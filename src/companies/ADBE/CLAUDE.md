# ADBE — Adobe Inc.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Segments.** Adobe reports three segments: Digital Media (Creative Cloud
  and Document Cloud), Digital Experience (Experience Cloud, analytics, and
  marketing solutions), and Publishing and Advertising (legacy products,
  declining). Through FY17 the segments were named Digital Media, Digital
  Marketing, and Print and Publishing; no restatement accompanied the rename.

- **Fiscal year.** Adobe uses a 52/53-week fiscal year ending the Friday
  nearest 30 November, so "FY25" covers the 52 weeks ending 28 November 2025. All figures in the data files use fiscal-year labels.

- **Subscription model.** The transition from perpetual licences to Creative
  Cloud subscriptions completed around FY16. Virtually all Digital Media and
  Digital Experience revenue is now subscription-based.

- **FY20 tax benefit.** The large negative income-tax line in FY20
  ($−1.1 B) reflects a one-time benefit from an intra-entity IP transfer
  under the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, which inflated net income that year
  well above operating income.

- **FY24 other operating.** The $1.2 B "other operating" charge in FY24
  consists primarily of the $1 B termination fee paid to Figma after the
  planned acquisition was abandoned.
