# PG — The Procter & Gamble Company

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Template choice.** P&G sells through retailers rather than its own stores,
  so the retail method's store, comparable-sales and inventory sections do not
  apply. The page uses the software template's general valuation, return and
  margin metrics, as the JNJ page does.

- **Fiscal year.** Procter & Gamble's fiscal year ends on 30 June, so "FY26"
  covers 1 July 2025 through 30 June 2026. The page covers FY18–FY26 plus
  FY27E. Year-end prices are the 30 June close (29 June in 2018 and 28 June in
  2024, the last trading days).

- **Segments.** Five reportable segments (Beauty; Grooming; Health Care;
  Fabric & Home Care; Baby, Feminine & Family Care) plus Corporate have been
  stable since FY18, so the segment chart needs no bridging. Corporate net
  sales are the residual of total net sales minus the five segments.

- **Impairments.** Shave Care and the Gillette brand were written down twice:
  $8.345B in FY19 (goodwill and indefinite-lived intangible) and $1.341B in
  FY24 (indefinite-lived intangible). Both sit in operating income on their own
  income statement line. The FY19 charge makes P/E, sustainable growth and cash
  earnings quality not meaningful, so those FY19 values are `null`, and the
  non-deductible part of it added 22.8 points to the FY19 effective tax rate.

- **Other one-off items.** FY18 includes a provisional $602M net charge from
  the US Tax Cuts and Jobs Act. FY25 other non-operating expense includes a
  $752M currency translation charge on substantially liquidating Argentina, and
  FY26 other non-operating income includes a $343M gain from dissolving the
  Glad joint venture.

- **Net income basis.** EPS, ROE, net margin and cash earnings quality use net
  earnings attributable to Procter & Gamble. The expense section's total uses
  net earnings including noncontrolling interests, so that operating expenses,
  non-operating items and taxes sum exactly to it. Equity excludes
  noncontrolling interests.

- **FY27E basis.** All forward figures come from fiscal 2027 guidance in the
  29 July 2026 earnings release. Revenue applies the 2% midpoint of 1%–3%
  all-in sales growth to FY26 net sales ($88.77B). EPS applies the 3% midpoint
  of 1%–5% GAAP EPS growth to $6.62 ($6.82). Free cash flow applies the 87.5%
  midpoint of the 85%–90% adjusted free cash flow productivity range to net
  earnings implied by that EPS at FY26 diluted shares (2.422B), giving $14.46B.
  Capex is 5% of guided net sales, the midpoint of 4.5%–5.5%. No consensus free
  cash flow was available from stockanalysis.com when the page was built.
  Replace FY27E with the FY27 10-K figures once filed (expected August 2027).
