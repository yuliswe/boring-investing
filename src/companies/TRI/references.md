## Notes

- Revenue and operating income for FY17–FY20 are reported on a continuing-
  operations basis, excluding the Financial & Risk business (divested 2018)
  and Intellectual Property & Science business (divested 2016).
- EPS figures for FY18 and FY21 are distorted by one-time gains from the
  Refinitiv and LSEG transactions respectively.
- P/FCF for FY19 is excluded (null) because restructuring costs under the
  Change Programme depressed free cash flow to $204M, making the ratio
  uninformative.
- ROE, ROIC, and D/E for FY17–FY20 are estimated from available public
  data and may differ from figures reported in filed statements.
- Segment revenue for FY20–FY25 comes from the company's earnings releases.
  The current five-segment structure dates from FY19 after the F&R divestiture.
