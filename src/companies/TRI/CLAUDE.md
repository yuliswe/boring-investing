# TRI — Thomson Reuters Corporation

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Segments.** Thomson Reuters reports five segments: Legal Professionals
  (Westlaw, Practical Law), Corporates (indirect tax, compliance, trade
  management for corporations and the Big Seven accounting firms), Tax, Audit &
  Accounting Professionals (ONESOURCE, UltraTax for smaller firms), Reuters News
  (agency and professional news), and Global Print (legacy print products in
  structural decline).

- **Fiscal year.** Thomson Reuters uses a calendar fiscal year ending
  31 December, so "FY25" covers January through December 2025.

- **Divestitures.** Thomson Reuters divested its Intellectual Property & Science
  business (now Clarivate) in 2016 and sold its Financial & Risk business to
  form Refinitiv in 2018, which was later acquired by LSEG. These divestitures
  mean that pre-FY19 figures reflect a substantially different business mix.
  FY18 and FY21 net income include large one-time gains from these transactions,
  which distort EPS, P/E, net margin, and related metrics in those years.

- **Recurring revenue.** More than eighty percent of revenue is recurring,
  primarily from software subscriptions and long-term information service
  contracts. The remaining revenue comes from transactions (filings, print) and
  the Global Print segment.

- **LSEG stake.** Thomson Reuters retains a significant equity stake in the
  London Stock Exchange Group, which can produce non-operating gains or losses
  that affect reported net income.
