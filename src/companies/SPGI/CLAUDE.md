# SPGI — S&P Global Inc.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Business model.** S&P Global provides credit ratings, benchmarks, analytics,
  and data to the capital and commodity markets through five divisions: Ratings,
  Market Intelligence, Indices, Energy, and Mobility. Revenue is predominantly
  subscription-based with strong pricing power derived from regulatory mandates
  (Ratings) and mission-critical workflows.

- **IHS Markit merger.** S&P Global completed the acquisition of IHS Markit on
  28 February 2022 in a ~$44 billion all-stock transaction. The merger added
  Market Intelligence breadth, the Mobility division, and the Energy (formerly
  Commodity Insights) division. Post-merger figures are not directly comparable
  to pre-merger years due to ~$1 billion per year in non-cash intangible
  amortization and an ~80 million increase in diluted shares from the stock-for-
  stock deal. The Engineering Solutions business was divested in FY22-FY23.

- **Mobility spin-off.** On 1 July 2026, S&P Global completed the spin-off of
  its Mobility division into an independent public company, Mobility Global
  (NYSE: MBGL). FY26 results include Mobility for H1 only. FY25 and prior years
  include Mobility for the full year.

- **Fiscal year.** S&P Global uses a calendar fiscal year ending 31 December, so
  "FY25" covers January through December 2025. All figures in the data files use
  fiscal-year labels.

- **Operating income definition.** SPGI's GAAP income statement reports
  "Operating Profit" after gains/losses on dispositions and equity in income on
  unconsolidated subsidiaries. The operating income figures in the data files use
  Revenue minus Total Expenses (before dispositions and equity income) to provide
  a cleaner view of recurring operating profitability. In FY22, a $1.9 billion
  gain on the sale of Engineering Solutions inflated GAAP Operating Profit; the
  Revenue minus Expenses definition excludes that gain.

- **Intangible amortization.** Post-merger GAAP earnings are depressed by
  ~$1 billion per year (~7% of revenue) in non-cash amortization of IHS Markit
  intangible assets. Free cash flow is a more representative measure of economic
  profitability than GAAP net income.

- **Segment structure.** Pre-merger (FY17-FY21): Ratings, Market Intelligence,
  Platts, and S&P Dow Jones Indices. Post-merger (FY22+): Market Intelligence,
  Ratings, Energy (renamed from Commodity Insights in FY24), Mobility, and
  Indices. The segment revenue chart shows the post-merger structure only.
