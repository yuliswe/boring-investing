# PEP — PepsiCo, Inc.

Analytical method: [retail](../../methods/retail.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Fiscal year.** PepsiCo uses a 52/53-week fiscal year ending on the last
  Saturday of December. "FY25" ended 27 December 2025. The dates shift by a
  day or two each year but are close enough to calendar years that the page
  labels them by calendar year.

- **Business mix.** PepsiCo is roughly half snack food (Frito-Lay, Quaker)
  and half beverages (Pepsi, Gatorade, SodaStream). This dual exposure means
  PepsiCo's margins, capital intensity, and growth profile differ from a
  pure-play beverage company like Coca-Cola or a pure-play snack company.

- **Segment reorganizations.** PepsiCo reorganized its reporting segments
  three times in the ten-year window:
  - FY16–FY17: six segments (FLNA, QFNA, NAB, LatAm, Europe Sub-Saharan
    Africa, AMENA).
  - FY18–FY23: seven segments (FLNA, QFNA, PBNA, LatAm, Europe, AMESA,
    APAC), adopted when North America Beverages was renamed PBNA and the
    international segments were reorganized.
  - FY24–FY25: six segments (PFNA, PBNA, IB Franchise, EMEA, LatAm Foods,
    Asia Pacific Foods), adopted when FLNA and QFNA merged into PepsiCo
    Foods North America and the international segments were restructured
    again.
    The segment revenue chart uses a simplified three-category view (North
    America Food, North America Beverages, International) that bridges all
    three structures.

- **FY17 tax charge.** The Tax Cuts and Jobs Act enacted in December 2017
  caused a provisional $2.5B net tax charge in FY17, depressing net income
  and EPS for that year.

- **FY18 tax benefit.** In FY18 PepsiCo recognized a net tax benefit of
  $3.4B from international reorganizations and the finalization of TCJA
  provisions, inflating net income to $12.5B (more than double a normal
  year) and making P/E artificially low at 12.5×.

- **FY25 impairment charges.** FY25 operating profit declined year-on-year
  despite revenue growth because PepsiCo recorded approximately $2.0B in
  other operating charges (impairment and restructuring), reducing GAAP
  operating profit to $11.5B.

- **Forward estimates.** FY26E uses consensus analyst estimates from
  stockanalysis.com (revenue $98.94B, core EPS $8.56, FCF $10.53B,
  operating income $15.89B, net income $10.96B). The forward P/E and P/FCF
  ratios recalculate dynamically when the user adjusts the stock price.
