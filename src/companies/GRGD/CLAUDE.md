# GRGD — Groupe Dynamite Inc.

Analytical method: [retail](../../methods/retail.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Listing and currency.** Groupe Dynamite is listed on the Toronto Stock
  Exchange as GRGD and reports in Canadian dollars under IFRS. It does not
  file with the SEC, so its filings come from SEDAR+ and the investor site.
  Every amount on the page, including the price, is in CA$.

- **Short public history.** The company listed in November 2024 through a
  purely secondary IPO at CA$21.00, in which the controlling shareholder
  sold shares and the company received no proceeds. Audited statements are
  available from FY21 (in the IPO prospectus), so the page has five actual
  years plus one estimate rather than nine. P/E and P/FCF exist only from
  FY24, and those two panels carry no median line.

- **Fiscal year.** The fiscal year ends on the Saturday closest to
  31 January, and the company names each year by the calendar year in which
  it **starts**, so "FY25" is the year ended 31 January 2026 and "FY26E"
  ends 30 January 2027. FY23 was a 53-week year. Aggregators such as
  MarketScreener label years by the end year instead.

- **Share consolidation.** At the IPO the old Class A shares were
  consolidated 4.249 to 1 into 107.5M multiple voting shares. The prospectus
  reports FY21 and FY22 EPS on the old count (CA$0.24 and CA$0.14), so the
  page divides net earnings by the post-consolidation 107.5M shares instead
  and says so in the year notes. FY23 EPS of CA$0.80 is the restated figure
  from the FY24 statements.

- **CCAA restructuring.** The company was under creditor protection from
  September 2020 to October 2021. FY21 carries a CA$104.7M gain on debt
  forgiveness (partly reversed by CA$12.2M in FY22), pandemic subsidies and
  unusually low lease costs, so FY21 net earnings and margins are not a
  usable baseline.

- **Presentation.** Cost of sales includes occupancy costs that IFRS 16
  leaves in operating expense, but all depreciation, including right-of-use
  depreciation, is a separate line below gross profit. Gross margin (about
  64%) is therefore not comparable with Aritzia, which books occupancy
  depreciation in cost of goods sold. Stock-based compensation sits inside
  SG&A.

- **Comparable sales exclude e-commerce.** The company's comparable store
  sales metric covers stores only. FY21 is measured against FY19, and from
  FY25 the metric uses budgeted exchange rates (FY25 constant-currency
  growth was 23.8% against the reported 26.7%).

- **Related-party flows before the IPO.** In FY22 the company borrowed to
  pay a CA$185M dividend and lend CA$110M to its parent. The note earned
  interest (in finance income) until the parent repaid it from IPO proceeds
  in FY24. Lease-adjusted ROIC subtracts the note from invested capital for
  FY22–FY23 because it was not an operating asset.

- **Forward estimates.** FY26E revenue (CA$1.651B) is FY25 revenue grown by
  the 26% midpoint of management guidance of 25–27% (10 September 2026).
  FY26E comparable store sales growth (13%), capital expenditures
  (CA$105M) and net store openings (9) are also guidance midpoints. Diluted
  EPS (CA$3.28), net income (CA$369.5M), EBIT (CA$542.4M), free cash flow
  (CA$333M) and the revenue used for margin estimates (CA$1.649B) are
  MarketScreener consensus. FY26E free cash flow after leases subtracts an
  assumed CA$38M of lease payments (the FY23–FY25 average) and divides by
  the first-half FY26 diluted share count of 114.0M. The company gives no
  gross margin guidance, so gross margin has no FY26E point. The forward P/E
  and P/FCF after leases recalculate when the user adjusts the price.
