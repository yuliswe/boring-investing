# GRGD — Data sources

All historical figures are taken from Groupe Dynamite's audited annual
financial statements, MD&As and IPO prospectus, published on
[investors.groupedynamite.com](https://investors.groupedynamite.com/) and on
SEDAR+. Amounts are in thousands of Canadian dollars in the filings and are
converted to billions on the page. Where a later filing presents a prior year
as a comparative, the later presentation is used.

## Operating metrics

Comparable store sales growth, store counts, openings and closures, and
retail sales per square foot come from the
[FY25 MD&A](https://investors.groupedynamite.com/download/Q4-25_MDA_EN.pdf),
the [FY24 MD&A](https://investors.groupedynamite.com/download/Q4_2024_MD-A_EN.pdf),
the [FY24 AIF](https://investors.groupedynamite.com/download/AIF_FY2024_EN.pdf),
and the prospectus MD&A for FY21–FY23. The FY21 inventory turnover (6.9×) is
the company's reported figure from the prospectus MD&A, because opening FY21
inventory is not in the filed statements.

## Derived metrics

- Free cash flow is operating cash flow minus additions to property and
  equipment and to intangible assets, which matches the company's own
  non-IFRS free cash flow.
- Free cash flow after leases is free cash flow minus repayment of principal
  on lease liabilities from the financing section of the cash flow
  statement. It drives P/FCF after leases, FCF per share after leases, the
  FCF margin after leases and FCF conversion.
- P/E and P/FCF use the TSX closing price on the last trading day of each
  fiscal year from Yahoo Finance (GRGD.TO), unadjusted for the December 2025
  special dividend: CA$16.15 on 31 January 2025 and CA$70.88 on
  30 January 2026.
- FY21 and FY22 diluted EPS and per-share free cash flow divide by the
  107,549,494 shares that resulted from the 4.249-to-1 consolidation at the
  IPO.
- Inventory turnover is cost of sales over the average of opening and
  closing inventory.
- Lease-adjusted ROIC is operating income times one minus the effective tax
  rate, over the average of opening and closing invested capital
  (shareholders' equity plus bank debt plus lease liabilities minus cash,
  less the CA$110M promissory note receivable from the parent in FY22–FY23,
  plus the CA$10.5M of notes payable to parent companies in FY24).
- Revenue per store is retail-channel revenue over the average of opening
  and closing store count. FY21 uses 303 stores at the end of FY20, from the
  prospectus MD&A.
- Net cash is cash minus the carrying value of long-term debt.
