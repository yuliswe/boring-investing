# ASML — ASML Holding N.V.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Currency and listing.** ASML reports in euros under US GAAP, and the page
  prices it on Euronext Amsterdam (ASML.AS) so that price and EPS share a
  currency. Historical P/E and P/FCF use the Euronext year-end close.

- **Filings.** ASML files an annual 20-F and furnishes quarterly results on
  Form 6-K, not a 10-K or 10-Q. FY16 and FY17 are shown as restated for
  ASC 606 in the FY18 20-F, except the FY16 cash-flow split between funds
  from operations and working capital, which uses the original statement.

- **Fiscal year.** The calendar year. Q3 2026 ended on 27 September 2026.

- **Bookings.** ASML stopped reporting quarterly bookings from Q1 2026, so
  the page has no 2026 bookings or backlog.

- **Other operating income.** FY16 and FY17 include payments under the 2012
  Customer Co-Investment Program, and FY21 includes a €0.21B gain on selling
  Berliner Glas subsidiaries. Stockanalysis excludes the FY21 gain from
  operating income; this page keeps it, as filed.

- **ROIC** is computed as operating income after tax divided by average
  equity plus debt minus cash and short-term investments, so customer down
  payments are not counted as capital. Debt to equity excludes leases.

- **Mistral AI.** The €1.3B stake bought in September 2025 is an equity
  security under the ASC 321 measurement alternative, not an equity-method
  investment. ASML revalued it in Q3 2026 for a €510M gain after the Series D
  round, and carried it at €2.24B on 27 September 2026.

- **Stress test.** The stress test removes AI hype and circular financing
  from FY25 and FY26E. It holds revenue to the low path of ASML's November
  2022 plan, published before ChatGPT, and splits the excess into a circular
  share and the remaining AI demand. Lost revenue comes out of EPS at a 55%
  decremental margin taxed at 17% (`LOST_PROFIT_SHARE`), the Q3 2026 Mistral
  gain is removed, and the Mistral stake is written off. FY24 is not stressed
  because its rise above the plan came from Chinese stockpiling. The reverse
  DCF carries a stressed free-cash-flow path built from the same lost profit.
  All AI and circular figures are estimates; the method is in
  `references.md`.
