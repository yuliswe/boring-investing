# CSU — Constellation Software Inc.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Business model.** Constellation Software is a serial acquirer of vertical
  market software (VMS) businesses. It operates a highly decentralized portfolio
  of hundreds of software companies across diverse industries including
  government, healthcare, utilities, and financial services. Organic growth is
  typically low single digits; revenue growth is primarily acquisition-driven.

- **Operating groups.** CSU operates through six autonomous groups: Volaris,
  Harris, Jonas, Perseus, Topicus (majority-owned, separately listed on TSX as
  TOI), and Lumine (majority-owned, separately listed on TSX as LMN). Segment
  revenue by operating group is not broken out in a consistent multi-year format
  in the consolidated filings.

- **Fiscal year.** Constellation's fiscal year ends 31 December, so "FY25"
  covers the calendar year 2025. All figures in the data files use fiscal-year
  labels.

- **Currency.** Financial statements are reported in US dollars, although the
  company is headquartered in Toronto and listed on the TSX in Canadian dollars.
  Stock prices in the hero section are in CAD.

- **IFRS reporting and intangible amortization.** CSU reports under IFRS, which
  requires amortization of acquired intangible assets. This large non-cash charge
  significantly depresses reported net income and GAAP EPS relative to cash
  earnings. The P/E ratio consequently appears elevated; P/FCF is a more
  representative valuation metric. The company's preferred profitability measure
  is "free cash flow available to shareholders" (FCFA2S).

- **Topicus spin-off (2021).** In February 2021, CSU completed the IPO of
  Topicus.com, distributing shares to CSU shareholders as a special dividend.
  CSU retains majority economic interest and continues to consolidate Topicus.
