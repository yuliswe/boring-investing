# LEN — Lennar Corporation

Analytical method: [homebuilder](../../methods/homebuilder.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Valuation metrics.** The page follows the homebuilder method rather than
  the software one. Critical Metrics shows price-to-tangible-book (P/TBV) and
  return on equity (ROE) instead of P/E and P/FCF, and three extra sections
  compare both against D.R. Horton, PulteGroup and NVR, now and from FY05 to
  FY25. The SoftwareTemplate is reused with its `criticalSection` and
  `keySection` overrides.

- **Fiscal year.** Lennar's fiscal year ends 30 November, so "FY25" is the
  year ended 30 November 2025. Year-end prices are the close on the last
  trading day on or before that date.

- **Share classes and per-share figures.** Lennar has Class A (LEN) and Class
  B (LEN.B) shares. Book value per share divides equity by both classes
  outstanding (issued minus treasury), and P/TBV uses the Class A price.

- **Unadjusted prices.** Yahoo's `close` series is adjusted for the November
  2017 Class B stock dividend and treats the February 2025 Millrose spin-off
  as a split. The page uses as-traded closes reconstructed from Yahoo's
  adjustment factors, which match the prices stated in the 10-Ks, so that
  each year's price lines up with that year's filed share count.

- **Goodwill.** Tangible book deducts total goodwill, which is $3.632B from
  FY20 onward ($3.442B Homebuilding, $0.190B Financial Services) and came
  mostly from the February 2018 CalAtlantic acquisition. Lennar reports no
  other intangible assets on its balance sheet.

- **Millrose spin-off (FY25).** Lennar spun off Millrose Properties in
  February 2025, reducing retained earnings by $4.84B, and in November 2025
  exchanged Millrose shares for 8.0M of its own Class A shares, recorded as a
  $1.0B treasury purchase and a $156.1M loss. Equity fell from $27.87B to
  $21.96B and tangible book per share from $90.36 to $74.20.

- **Operating income.** Lennar does not report operating income. The revenue
  chart's operating line is total revenues less total costs and expenses,
  before equity earnings, other income and technology investment results.
  No consensus estimate exists on that basis, so FY26E is blank on that line.

- **Segments.** The FY2019 10-K recast FY17 and FY18 into the current four
  segments, folding Rialto into Lennar Other after the FY18 sale of the Rialto
  platform. The Homebuilding cost breakdown for FY17 is from the FY2018 10-K
  and sums to $9.752B against the recast $9.743B.

- **Peer data.** NVR is excluded from the two through-the-cycle charts
  because its P/TBV (1.9x to 8.3x) and ROE (8% to 92%) would flatten the
  other lines; it appears in the peer panels. NVR's tangible book deducts its
  $48.9M excess reorganization value, assumed unchanged in FY25 after the
  10-K stopped showing it separately. D.R. Horton's figures exclude the
  Forestar noncontrolling interest.

- **Full-cycle ROE.** The page averages FY06–FY25. Lennar's average (7.0%) is
  far below its median (13.4%) because of losses equal to 41%, 34% and 17%
  of equity in FY07–FY09.
