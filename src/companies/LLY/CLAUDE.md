# LLY — Eli Lilly and Company

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Business model.** Eli Lilly is a global pharmaceutical company that
  discovers, develops, manufactures, and markets medicines across several
  therapeutic areas. Revenue comes from product sales — the company does not
  operate a services or platform business. Margins are driven by the gap between
  manufacturing cost and the pricing power of patented drugs.

- **Segments.** Lilly does not report operating segments by therapeutic area in
  the way a technology company reports by product line. Instead, revenue is
  disclosed by individual product. Key products include Mounjaro and Zepbound
  (tirzepatide, for diabetes and obesity), Verzenio (oncology), Jardiance
  (diabetes), Taltz (immunology), and legacy insulins (Humalog, Humulin).

- **Fiscal year.** Calendar fiscal year ending 31 December, so "FY25" covers
  January through December 2025.

- **Elanco spin-off.** Lilly completed the spin-off of its animal health
  business, Elanco Animal Health, in early 2019. FY18 and earlier revenue
  figures include animal health; FY19 onward reflects continuing pharmaceutical
  operations only. The FY19 income statement includes a ~$3.7B gain on the
  Elanco disposition reported below operating income.

- **FY17 TCJA charge.** GAAP net income for FY17 was a loss of $204M, driven
  by a $2.4B provisional charge for the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act (repatriation
  toll tax on accumulated foreign earnings). Underlying operating performance
  was profitable.

- **Acquired IPR&D charges.** Lilly regularly acquires pipeline assets and
  expenses acquired in-process research and development immediately. These
  charges can be large — $3.8B in FY23 and $3.3B in FY24 — and depress
  reported operating income relative to the underlying business.

- **Manufacturing capacity expansion.** Beginning in FY22, Lilly dramatically
  increased capital expenditures to build manufacturing capacity for
  tirzepatide (Mounjaro/Zepbound), growing capex from $1.9B in FY22 to $7.8B
  in FY25.
