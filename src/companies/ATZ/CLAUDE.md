# ATZ — Aritzia Inc.

Analytical method: [retail](../../methods/retail.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Listing and currency.** Aritzia is listed on the Toronto Stock Exchange
  as ATZ and reports in Canadian dollars under IFRS. It does not file with
  the SEC, so its filings come from SEDAR+ and the investor site rather than
  EDGAR. Every amount on the page, including the price, is in CA$.

- **Fiscal year.** Aritzia uses a 52/53-week fiscal year ending on the
  Sunday closest to the end of February. The company names each fiscal year
  by the calendar year in which it ends, so "FY26" is the year ended
  1 March 2026. FY19 and FY24 were 53-week years.

- **IFRS 16.** Aritzia adopted IFRS 16 at the start of FY20 without
  restating FY18 and FY19. Rent was replaced by depreciation of right-of-use
  assets (inside cost of goods sold) and interest on lease liabilities
  (inside finance expense), and lease principal payments moved from
  operating to financing cash flow. Operating cash flow and free cash flow
  from FY20 onward are therefore higher than they would have been under the
  old standard, and the cost-analysis lines shift in FY20.

- **Occupancy in cost of goods sold.** Aritzia books boutique occupancy and
  distribution costs in cost of goods sold, so its gross margin (about 45%)
  is not directly comparable with retailers that report rent in SG&A.

- **Stock-based compensation line.** The statement of operations shows
  stock-based compensation as its own line below SG&A. The cost analysis
  keeps it separate. Other expense (income) includes fair-value changes on
  equity derivative contracts that hedge cash-settled share units, which
  produced large gains in FY25 and FY26.

- **Comparable sales.** Aritzia did not report comparable sales growth for
  FY21 or FY22 because pandemic closures broke the comparable base, so
  those years are blank.

- **Boutique count.** The count excludes the four Reigning Champ boutiques
  that came with the FY22 acquisition of CYC Design Corporation.

- **Forward estimates.** FY27E revenue (CA$4.65B) is the midpoint of
  management guidance of CA$4.55–4.75B and FY27E gross margin (46.9%) is the
  midpoint of the guided 175–225 bp increase, both from the Q1 FY27 release
  on 9 July 2026. FY27E capital expenditures (CA$250M) are also guidance.
  Diluted EPS (CA$4.69), net income (CA$562.2M), free cash flow (CA$637.7M)
  and the revenue used for margin estimates (CA$4.71B) are MarketScreener
  consensus. No comparable forward estimate of income from operations is
  available, because the consensus EBIT excludes stock-based compensation.
  The forward P/E and P/FCF recalculate when the user adjusts the price.
