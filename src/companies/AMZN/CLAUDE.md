# AMZN — Amazon.com, Inc.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Business model.** Amazon operates the world's largest e-commerce marketplace
  (first-party retail and third-party seller services), the leading cloud
  infrastructure platform (AWS), and a growing digital advertising business.
  Revenue is earned from product sales, seller commissions, fulfillment services,
  cloud computing, advertising, and Prime subscriptions.

- **Segments.** Amazon reports three segments: North America (online and physical
  retail, advertising, subscriptions in the US, Canada, and Mexico),
  International (same businesses outside North America), and Amazon Web Services
  (cloud computing, storage, database, and AI/ML services).

- **Fiscal year.** Calendar fiscal year ending 31 December, so "FY25" covers
  January through December 2025.

- **Stock split.** A 20:1 stock split became effective 6 June 2022. All
  per-share data and stock prices on the page are split-adjusted. Pre-split
  diluted shares (493M–515M for FY17–FY21) are shown as 9,860M–10,300M.

- **FY2022 net loss.** The $2.7B GAAP net loss was driven by $16.8B in
  unrealised losses on the Rivian equity investment (Rivian IPO November 2021,
  Amazon held approximately 158M shares whose price fell roughly 80% during
  2022). Underlying operations were profitable, with operating income of $12.2B.

- **Equity-investment gains and losses.** FY21 other income of $14.6B and FY25
  other income of $15.2B include large unrealised gains on equity investments
  (Rivian in FY21, Anthropic and others in FY25). These gains cause net income
  to exceed operating income in those years. FY22 other expense of $16.8B
  reflects the reversal on Rivian.

- **Capex surge.** Capital expenditures grew from $12.0B in FY17 to $131.8B in
  FY25, driven primarily by AI data-centre infrastructure for AWS. FY25 capex
  of $131.8B nearly matched operating cash flow of $139.5B, compressing free
  cash flow to $7.7B. FY26E consensus expects capex to exceed operating cash
  flow, producing negative free cash flow.

- **Label renames.** "Technology and content" was renamed to "Technology and
  infrastructure" starting with the FY2023 filing. "Marketing" was renamed to
  "Sales and marketing" starting with the FY2022 filing. The page uses the
  current labels for all years.
