# SU — Data Sources

All filings are on SEC EDGAR under CIK 0000311337.

## Annual figures

- Income statements, balance sheets and cash flow statements: audited
  consolidated financial statements in the 40-F filings for FY25 (filed
  26 February 2026), FY23 (22 March 2024), FY21 (25 February 2022), FY19
  (28 February 2020) and FY18 (1 March 2019), each giving two years. FY17 uses
  the restated comparatives in the FY18 filing.
- Segment gross revenues: the segmented information note in the same filings.
- Adjusted funds from operations, capital expenditures excluding capitalized
  interest, royalties, gross revenues for FY17–FY21, net debt as Suncor
  defines it, production, cash operating costs per barrel, refinery throughput
  and utilization, and average WTI prices: the annual MD&A for FY19 (40-F filed
  28 February 2020), FY22 (40-F filed 7 March 2023) and FY25 (40-F filed
  26 February 2026), each covering three years.
- Retail station count (1,732 in Canada at 31 December 2025): 2025 annual
  information form.
- Year-end share prices: Yahoo Finance daily closes for SU.TO on the last
  trading day of each year.
