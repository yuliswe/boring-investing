# MCD — McDonald's Corporation

Analytical method: [retail](../../methods/retail.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Fiscal year.** McDonald's uses a calendar fiscal year ending 31 December,
  so "FY25" covers January through December 2025.

- **Franchise model.** Roughly 95% of McDonald's restaurants are franchised.
  The company earns rent and royalties from franchisees, making it more of a
  real-estate and brand-licensing business than a traditional restaurant
  operator. Company-operated restaurant revenue is a minority of total
  revenue and carries materially lower margins than the franchise stream.

- **Negative equity.** McDonald's has had negative shareholders' equity since
  FY16 due to cumulative share buybacks exceeding retained earnings. This
  makes ROE and debt-to-equity mechanically meaningless, so the page shows
  ROIC instead of ROE and omits D/E entirely.

- **Refranchising.** From FY16 to FY18 McDonald's refranchised thousands of
  restaurants, which reduced reported revenue (company-operated restaurant
  revenue converts to lower franchise fees) while improving operating margins.
  Revenue and expense trends across those years reflect the structural shift,
  not organic decline.

- **Segment reorganization.** McDonald's adopted the current geographic
  segment structure (US, International Operated Markets, International
  Developmental Licensed Markets & Corporate) in FY17. Prior years used
  a different grouping (ILM, High Growth, Foundational Markets & Corporate),
  so the segment revenue section begins at FY17.

- **FY22 Russia exit.** The FY22 "Other operating (income)/expense" line
  includes a charge of approximately $1B from the sale of the McDonald's
  Russia business to a local licensee.
