## Operational metrics

- Systemwide restaurant count, comp sales, franchised %: 10-K annual reports,
  "Systemwide Restaurants" and "Comparable Sales" sections
- Franchise margin: computed from franchised restaurant revenue and franchised
  restaurants occupancy expenses as reported in each 10-K income statement

## Cash flow

- Operating cash flow, CapEx, cash taxes paid, working capital changes:
  10-K cash flow statements and stockanalysis.com/stocks/mcd/financials/cash-flow-statement/
- D&A and SBC: 10-K cash flow statement supplemental disclosures
