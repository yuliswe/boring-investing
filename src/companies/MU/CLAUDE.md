# MU — Micron Technology, Inc.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Fiscal year.** Micron uses a 52/53-week fiscal year ending on the Thursday
  closest to 31 August, so "FY26" covers 29 August 2025 through 3 September
  2026 (a 53-week year). All figures use Micron's fiscal-year labels.

- **FY26 source.** The FY26 10-K had not been filed when this page was built
  (October 2026), so FY26 figures come from the Q4 FY26 earnings release
  (8-K exhibit 99.1, filed 30 September 2026), which carries the full-year
  income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement. Replace them with
  the 10-K figures once it is filed, and check for any audit adjustments.

- **Cyclicality.** Memory is a commodity business with three major DRAM
  suppliers (Micron, Samsung, SK hynix). Margins swing with industry pricing:
  operating margin was 49% in FY18, −37% in FY23, and 75% in FY26. P/E and
  P/FCF are therefore lowest near cycle peaks, and several years have
  near-zero or negative earnings or free cash flow. Those years are shown as
  `null` with a `yearNotes` explanation rather than as extreme ratios.

- **Revenue breakdown.** The Revenue Streams section uses revenue by
  technology (DRAM, NAND, Other), which Micron discloses in every 10-K. The
  business units were reorganized in FY26 into Cloud Memory, Core Data Center,
  Mobile and Client, and Automotive and Embedded, so business-unit history is
  not comparable across ten years. FY26 technology revenue is the Q1–Q3 10-Q
  year-to-date amount plus the Q4 amount from the earnings call prepared
  remarks (rounded to $0.1B), with Other as the residual.

- **Free cash flow.** The page uses operating cash flow minus gross
  expenditures for property, plant, and equipment. Micron's own "adjusted free
  cash flow" also nets proceeds from government incentives (CHIPS Act and
  others) and sales of equipment, which makes it higher (FY26: $62.31B versus
  $58.96B here).

- **Customer prepayments.** In FY26 Micron signed strategic customer
  agreements and received $12.75B of customer contract liability deposits,
  which it classifies as financing cash inflows.

- **Expense lines.** "Other operating (income) expense, net" is computed as
  revenue minus cost of goods sold, R&D, SG&A, and operating income, because
  the XBRL `OtherOperatingIncomeExpenseNet` tag covers only part of that line.
  "Equity-method and noncontrolling interests" is pre-tax income minus tax
  minus net income attributable to Micron.
