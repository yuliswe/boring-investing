# UBER — Data Sources

## Consensus estimates

- FY26E diluted EPS ($2.95) and free cash flow ($10.49B) are consensus
  analyst estimates from stockanalysis.com (49 analysts, accessed
  22 Sep 2026). Free cash flow per share ($5.12) is derived from the
  consensus FCF divided by approximately 2.05B diluted shares (H1 2026
  weighted average). Uber does not report a PEG ratio because GAAP EPS
  growth is negative year-over-year, since FY24 and FY25 included large
  deferred tax asset valuation allowance releases that inflated those years.
- The forward P/E and P/FCF ratios use these consensus estimates with the
  default stock price, and recalculate reactively when the price adjuster
  changes.

## Reverse DCF

- Shares outstanding (2.04B): stockanalysis.com, October 2026, <https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/uber/>
- Net cash (-7.33B): $12.72B of debt minus $5.39B of cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments, Q2 2026 10-Q; about $12.5B of equity stakes are given no value
- Starting value: Consensus FY26E free cash flow of $10.49B, as in the Cash Flow section.
