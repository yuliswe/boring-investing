# JNJ — Johnson & Johnson

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Fiscal year.** Johnson & Johnson's fiscal year ends on the Sunday closest
  to 31 December, so "FY25" covers 30 December 2024 through 28 December 2025.
  FY20 was a 53-week year.

- **Kenvue break.** The Consumer Health business was separated as Kenvue in
  2023 (initial public offering, then the exchange offer on 23 August). The FY23–FY25 10-Ks restate
  FY21 onward to continuing operations, while FY17–FY20 are only available as
  originally filed, including Consumer Health. The income statement, expense
  lines, and segment chart therefore change basis between FY20 and FY21.
  The cash flow statement was never restated and stays consolidated through
  FY23. The FY21 balance sheet was not restated either, so FY21 days sales
  outstanding uses consolidated revenue ($93.775B).

- **Net income basis.** P/E, EPS, ROE, and sustainable growth use total net
  earnings for FY17–FY22, when shareholders still owned Consumer Health and
  equity included it, and continuing operations from FY23, which excludes the
  $21.0B one-time gain on the Kenvue exchange offer. Net margin and the
  revenue chart use continuing operations throughout, matching restated
  revenue.

- **Operating income.** Johnson & Johnson does not report an operating income
  subtotal. The page defines it as revenue minus cost of products sold,
  selling, marketing and administrative expenses, R&D, in-process R&D, and
  restructuring. Interest and "Other (income) expense, net" are treated as
  non-operating, which reconciles exactly to reported pre-tax earnings in
  every year.

- **Talc litigation.** Talc accruals sit in "Other (income) expense, net":
  about $7.0B charged in FY23, $5.1B in FY24, and about $7.0B of reserve
  reversed in FY25. They drive most of the swing in net earnings.

- **FY17 tax charge.** A provisional Tax Cuts and Jobs Act charge of about
  $13.0B cut FY17 net earnings to $1.3B and EPS to $0.47, so FY17 P/E,
  sustainable growth, and cash earnings quality are shown as `null`.

- **FY26E basis.** Revenue is the midpoint of reported-sales guidance
  ($101.1B, July 2026). EPS is the midpoint of adjusted (non-GAAP) EPS guidance
  ($11.68), because the company does not guide GAAP EPS and no reliable GAAP
  consensus was available, so FY26E P/E is not comparable with the GAAP
  history. Free cash flow is the stockanalysis.com consensus ($25.15B).
  Replace FY26E with the FY26 10-K figures once filed (expected February 2027).

- **Orthopaedics separation.** Announced in October 2025, targeted for
  completion 18–24 months later. When it completes, expect another restatement
  of MedTech and the income statement.
