# JNJ — Data Sources

## SEC filings

- 10-K annual reports (consolidated statements of earnings, cash flows, and
  balance sheets, plus segment sales detail)
  <https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000200406&type=10-K>
  - FY2025 10-K (filed 11 February 2026): FY23–FY25
  - FY2024 10-K (filed 13 February 2025): FY22 restated
  - FY2023 10-K (filed 16 February 2024): FY21 restated, Kenvue exchange offer
  - FY2022 10-K (filed 16 February 2023): FY21 balance sheet as filed
  - FY2020 10-K (filed 22 February 2021): FY18–FY20, segment sales FY18–FY20
  - FY2019 10-K (filed 18 February 2020): FY17, segment sales FY17
  - FY2018 10-K (filed 20 February 2019): FY17 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act charge,
    Actelion acquisition
- Q2 2026 earnings release (8-K exhibit 99.1, filed 15 July 2026): full-year
  2026 guidance
  <https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/200406/000020040626000146/a2026q2exhibit991.htm>
- Q2 2026 10-Q (filed 23 July 2026): first-half 2026 diluted shares (2,443.9M)

## XBRL data

- SEC EDGAR XBRL companyfacts API (CIK 0000200406), used to cross-check:
  RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax,
  EarningsPerShareDiluted, WeightedAverageNumberOfDilutedSharesOutstanding,
  ShortTermBorrowings, LongTermDebtNoncurrent, AccountsReceivableNetCurrent,
  PaymentsOfOrdinaryDividends, CommonStockDividendsPerShareCashPaid
