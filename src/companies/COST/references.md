# COST — Data sources

Historical figures come from Costco's annual reports on Form 10-K, read
through the SEC's XBRL company facts
([CIK 0000909832](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0000909832.json))
and checked against the filings on
[EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000909832&type=10-K).
Where a later filing restates a year, the later figure is used.

## Financial statements

Total revenue, merchandise costs, SG&A, operating income, interest expense,
interest income and other (net), provision for income taxes, net income
including and attributable to Costco, diluted EPS, diluted weighted average
shares, the cash flow statement lines, merchandise inventories, accounts
payable, cash and cash equivalents, short-term investments, long-term debt
(current and noncurrent), total Costco stockholders' equity and operating
lease liabilities come from the 10-Ks for fiscal 2017 to fiscal 2026.
Membership fees and net sales come from the income statement. FY18 and FY19
preopening expenses are from the FY20 10-K.

## Segment and category revenue

- Total revenue by operating segment (United States, Canada, Other
  International) comes from the segment note: FY18–FY20 from the FY20 10-K,
  FY21–FY22 from the FY23 10-K, FY23 from the FY25 10-K and FY24–FY26 from
  the FY26 10-K.
- Net sales by merchandise category come from the disaggregated revenue
  note: FY18 from the FY20 10-K (Hardlines plus Softlines as Non-Foods),
  FY19–FY20 from the FY21 10-K, FY21–FY23 from the FY23 10-K and FY24–FY26
  from the FY26 10-K.

## Operating metrics

Comparable sales growth (headline and excluding gasoline prices and foreign
exchange), paid members, Executive members, renewal rates, warehouse counts
and operating floor space come from Items 1, 2 and 7 of each 10-K. Executive
members for FY18–FY20 are from the five-year table in the FY20 10-K. The FY17
year-end count of 741 warehouses, and FY17 inventory ($9.83B) and net sales
($126.17B), are the bases for FY18 calculations.

## Derived metrics

- Free cash flow is operating cash flow minus capital expenditures (additions
  to property and equipment). Under US GAAP, operating lease payments stay in
  operating cash flow, so no lease adjustment is made.
- P/E and P/FCF use the Nasdaq closing price on the last trading day of each
  fiscal year, from Yahoo Finance, divided by diluted EPS and by free cash
  flow per diluted share.
- Lease-adjusted ROIC is operating income × (1 − effective tax rate) divided
  by the average of opening and closing invested capital (Costco
  stockholders' equity plus long-term debt plus operating lease liabilities
  minus cash and cash equivalents). It starts in FY21 because ASC 842 was
  adopted in FY20.
- Net cash is cash and cash equivalents plus short-term investments minus
  long-term debt including the current portion.
- Net borrowings are proceeds from long-term and short-term borrowings less
  repayments. Other financing is total financing less repurchases, dividends
  and net borrowings.
