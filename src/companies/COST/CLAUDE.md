# COST — Costco Wholesale Corporation

Analytical method: [retail](../../methods/retail.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Fiscal year.** Costco uses a 52/53-week fiscal year ending on the Sunday
  nearest the end of August, and names each year by the calendar year in
  which it ends, so "FY26" is the year ended 30 August 2026. FY23 had 53
  weeks, and comparable sales exclude the extra week. Quarters are twelve
  weeks, except the sixteen-week fourth quarter.

- **Revenue is net sales plus membership fees.** Total revenue, operating
  margin, net margin, FCF margin and capex share use total revenue. Gross
  margin uses net sales only, following Costco's own convention, so it is
  merchandise margin before fee income. Membership fees ÷ operating income is
  added to the pricing group because fees, not markups, carry the profit.

- **Metric substitutions.** The retail method's "digital share of revenue" is
  replaced by paid members, Executive members and the U.S. and Canada renewal
  rate, because Costco reports e-commerce on changing definitions (e-commerce
  comparable sales until FY22, "digitally-enabled" comparable sales in the
  FY26 10-K) and
  membership is the better test of the franchise. The growth market is the
  Other International segment. Revenue per average store uses consolidated
  net sales, which include e-commerce and gasoline, because Costco does not
  report warehouse-only sales.

- **Comparable sales are whole percentages.** Costco reports total-company
  comps rounded to the nearest percent, both headline and excluding gasoline
  prices and foreign exchange. The renewal rate was also reported to whole
  percentages until FY22.

- **Membership count changes.** At the end of FY20 Costco standardized its
  count method worldwide, adding about 1.3M paid members. The FY21 10-K did
  not disclose the number of Executive members, so that point is null.

- **Category regrouping.** In FY21 Costco merged Hardlines and Softlines into
  Non-Foods. FY18 Non-Foods is the sum of the two former categories; FY19 and
  FY20 are as restated in the FY21 10-K.

- **Preopening expenses.** Costco reported preopening as a separate line
  until FY21 and now includes it in SG&A, with prior years restated from
  FY20. FY18 and FY19 SG&A add the preopening line ($68M and $86M) so the
  series is consistent.

- **Special dividends.** Costco returns surplus cash through special
  dividends rather than buybacks: $10 a share in December 2020 (FY21), $15 in
  January 2024 (FY24) and $30 declared on 10 November 2026, payable
  4 December 2026 (FY27, about $13.3B). The reverse DCF keeps net cash at the
  30 August 2026 balance because the 20 November price is cum-dividend.

- **Forward estimates.** Costco gives no revenue, earnings or cash flow
  guidance, so FY27E is the stockanalysis.com consensus (updated 17 November
  2026): revenue $329.86B, operating income $12.97B, net income $10.15B,
  diluted EPS $22.80 (35 analysts) and free cash flow $7.61B. Free cash flow
  per share ($17.12) divides consensus free cash flow by FY26 diluted shares
  (444.4M). The forward P/E and P/FCF recalculate when the user adjusts the
  price.
