# NOW — ServiceNow, Inc.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Segments.** ServiceNow reports a single operating segment. The Revenue
  Streams section splits revenue by type instead, into subscription and
  professional services and other, as the income statement does.

- **Fiscal year.** ServiceNow's fiscal year is the calendar year, so "FY25"
  covers January through December 2025.

- **Stock split.** A five-for-one split took effect on 17 December 2025. The
  FY25 10-K restates FY23 to FY25 per-share figures; earlier years in the data
  files are the as-filed figures divided by five. Year-end prices from Yahoo
  Finance are already split-adjusted.

- **GAAP versus non-GAAP.** The page uses GAAP figures throughout. ServiceNow
  and most analysts quote non-GAAP EPS (about $3.51 for FY25 against GAAP
  $1.67), which excludes stock-based compensation of about 15% of revenue and
  amortization of acquired intangibles. The consensus EPS shown on
  stockanalysis.com is non-GAAP, so FY26E GAAP EPS is derived from consensus
  GAAP net income instead. Free cash flow here is operating cash flow minus
  capital expenditures, whereas ServiceNow's own measure also adds back
  acquisition-related costs.

- **Tax valuation allowance releases.** FY19 ($0.56B) and FY23 ($0.72B)
  income tax lines are benefits from releasing valuation allowances on
  deferred tax assets, which inflate net income, EPS, ROE and net margin in
  those years.

- **2026 acquisitions.** ServiceNow bought Veza (about $1.2B, March 2026) and
  Armis (about $7.6B, April 2026), after Moveworks ($2.4B, December 2025). The
  cash came from a $4.0B term loan, refinanced in May 2026 with $4.0B of
  senior notes, and commercial paper. Debt rose from $1.49B at FY25 to $7.52B
  at Q3 2026, and the guided FY26 GAAP operating margin fell to 10%.

- **Default price.** The default price is an intraday quote from 29 October
  2026, the day after Q3 results, when the stock was down about 25%. Update
  it to a close when the page is next refreshed.
