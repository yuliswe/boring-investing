# CNQ — Canadian Natural Resources Limited

Analytical method: [energy](../../methods/energy.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Listing and currency.** Canadian Natural is listed on the Toronto Stock
  Exchange and the NYSE as CNQ and reports in Canadian dollars under IFRS. As
  a foreign private issuer it files its annual report on Form 40-F and its
  quarterly reports on Form 6-K with the SEC. Every amount on the page,
  including the price, is in CA$ and uses the TSX close.

- **Fiscal year.** The fiscal year is the calendar year, so "FY25" is the
  year ended 31 December 2025.

- **Share split.** A two-for-one split took effect on 11 June 2024. The FY24
  and FY25 40-Fs restate FY22 and FY23 per-share figures; FY17–FY21 EPS,
  share counts and FY17–FY22 dividends per share are the as-filed figures
  halved. Year-end prices are Yahoo's split-adjusted `close`, with Yahoo's
  1.021 adjustment for the August 2022 special dividend removed, so they are
  the as-traded price divided by two before 11 June 2024.

- **Revenue and operating income.** Revenue is product sales less royalties,
  as reported. Canadian Natural presents expenses in one list with no
  operating income subtotal. The page's operating income is revenue minus
  production, transportation, blending and feedstock, depletion,
  administration, share-based compensation and ARO accretion. FY24 and FY25
  report blending and feedstock separately from transportation; the page adds
  them together to keep the series comparable.

- **FY17 restatement.** FY17 revenue, production expense and transportation
  are as restated in the FY19 40-F for IFRS 15, which grossed up product
  sales and the related costs by the same amount.

- **Free cash flow.** Operating cash flow minus net expenditures on property,
  plant and equipment and on exploration and evaluation assets. Acquisitions
  (AOSP 2017, Devon 2019, Chevron 2024, AOSP asset swap 2025) are excluded.
  Under IFRS 16 lease payments sit in financing, so lease liabilities are
  included in net debt for the reverse DCF.

- **FY25 one-off items.** The 1 November 2025 AOSP asset swap with Shell gave
  a CA$4.99B pre-tax gain (CA$3.83B after tax), mostly the non-cash
  remeasurement of the existing 90% interest in the mines. Depletion includes
  CA$1.78B of non-cash charges for North Sea decommissioning costs, the Espoir
  licence and the Kossipo exploration asset. Adjusted net earnings from
  operations (CA$7.44B) are net earnings minus the CA$3.38B of after-tax
  non-operating items disclosed in the MD&A.

- **Forward estimates.** FY26E EPS (CA$6.55, 12 analysts) is the consensus
  of adjusted EPS, since the GAAP-basis figure on stockanalysis.com carries
  no analyst count. Revenue (CA$49.18B, 7 analysts) and free cash flow
  (CA$13.31B) are also stockanalysis.com consensus. FY26E net earnings are
  consensus EPS times FY25 diluted shares. Production (1,659.5 MBOE/d),
  capital expenditures (CA$6.115B) and acquisitions (CA$1.526B) are
  management guidance from 6 August 2026. Dividends per share annualize the
  current CA$0.625 quarterly dividend. Q3 2026 results are due on
  5 November 2026.
