# AMD — Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.

Analytical method: [software](../../methods/software.md)

## Company-specific notes

- **Fiscal year.** AMD uses a 52/53-week fiscal year ending on the last
  Saturday in December, so "FY25" covers the year ended 27 December 2025.
  FY22 was a 53-week year. Year-end prices are the close on the last trading
  day on or before the fiscal year-end date.

- **Segment structure.** From FY22 to FY24 AMD reported four segments (Data
  Center, Client, Gaming, Embedded), recast back to FY21. In FY25 it merged
  Client and Gaming into one reporting segment but still discloses revenue for
  each market, so the page keeps four series. Before FY21 the segments were
  Computing and Graphics and Enterprise, Embedded and Semi-Custom, which do not
  map onto the current markets; those figures are quoted in the chart note
  rather than charted.

- **Xilinx acquisition.** AMD acquired Xilinx in February 2022 for $48.5B in
  stock. Equity rose from $7.5B to $54.8B, which collapses ROE and ROIC from
  FY22 onward, and amortization of acquired intangibles ($2.3B to $3.5B a
  year, split between cost of sales and operating expenses) depresses GAAP
  earnings. The cost analysis combines both amortization lines into one.

- **Tax benefits.** FY20 net income includes a $1.3B release of the valuation
  allowance on deferred tax assets. FY25 includes a $793M benefit from
  resolving uncertain tax positions, producing a negative tax provision.

- **ZT Systems.** AMD acquired ZT Systems in FY25 and divested its data center
  manufacturing business in the same year. FY25 net income includes $66M from
  discontinued operations, and FY25 operating and investing cash flows include
  $1.216B and $1.318B from discontinued operations respectively.

- **Forward EPS.** Consensus EPS on stockanalysis.com is non-GAAP. To keep the
  P/E series on a GAAP basis, FY26E EPS is consensus net income divided by the
  latest diluted share count from the 10-Q.

- **Licensing gain.** Royalties from the THATIC joint venture appear as a
  reduction of operating expenses in FY17, FY19, FY21 and FY22. The equity
  income line is a different joint venture (ATMP, with Tongfu
  Microelectronics).
